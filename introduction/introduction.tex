The LSST Camera (LSSTCam) is the largest digital camera in the world.

LSSTCam was delivered to the Vera C. Rubin Observatory atop Cerro Pachón in Chile in 2024. Camera re-verification activities were subsequently carried out, as documented in \citet{SITCOMTN-148}.

This technote describes the commissioning of LSSTCam on the telescope, with a focus on camera hardware.  It covers 2025 and the first nine months of 2026, up to the closure of the summit ahead of the storm of July 2026; the storm and the recovery from it are the subject of a separate document. Engineering first light through the telescope, marking the first photons detected by the camera on the telescope, was achieved on April 15, 2025. Throughout the year, we encountered and addressed a number of technical challenges and implemented various improvements.

The technote is organized as follows:

Section \ref{sec:introduction} provides an overview of the events and issues encountered during commissioning. Section \ref{sec:issues} is constructed by Claude based on all the message exchanges in Rubin Observatory Slack channels \#cam-summit-ops, \#cam-operator and \#cam-filter-exchange. 

Section \ref{sec:operations} describes selected topics in greater detail.

Section \ref{sec:optimizations} reports on activities related to system optimization.

Section \ref{sec:performance} documents LSSTCam performance based on on-sky observations.

A chronology based on the Slack message exchange is also available in Appendix \ref{sec:chronology}.
