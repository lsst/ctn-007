The LSST Camera is the largest digital camera in the world.

The LSST Camera was delivered to the Vera C. Rubin Observatory atop Cerro Pachón in Chile in 2024. Camera re-verification activities were subsequently carried out, as documented in \citet{SITCOMTN-148}.

This technote describes the commissioning of the LSST Camera on the telescope in 2025, with a focus on camera hardware. Engineering first light through the telescope, marking the first photons detected by the camera on the telescope, was achieved on April 15, 2025. Throughout the year, we encountered and addressed a number of technical challenges and implemented various improvements.

The technote is organized as follows:

Section \ref{sec:introduction} provides an overview of the events and issues encountered during commissioning. Section \ref{sec:issues-2025} is constructed by Claude based on all the message exchanges in Rubin Obs Slack channels \#cam-summit-ops and \#cam-operator. 

Section \ref{sec:operations} describes selected topics in greater detail.

Section \ref{sec:optimizations} reports on activities related to system optimization.

Section \ref{sec:performance} documents camera performance based on on-sky observations.

Chronology based on the Slack message exchange is also available in Appendix \ref{sec:chronology-2025}.
